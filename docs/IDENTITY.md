# Universal Entropics — Special Systems Section

![Universal Entropics, Special Systems Section](images/ue-logo.svg)

*From the house-style sheet issued with the Duwamish, Seattle, 1967.*

The **Special Systems Section** of Universal Entropics designed and built
the Duwamish. It was a small group in the company's Seattle works on the
river of the same name. The committee of consultants reported to it, and
the machine carries its name on the console, the manuals and the listings.

The identity follows the corporate programmes of the day: Paul Rand's
IBM and Westinghouse marks, and Chermayeff & Geismar's work for Chase
Manhattan and Mobil. It uses one mark, one typeface, one ink and one
accent, applied the same way everywhere.

## The mark

<img src="images/ue-mark.svg" width="120" alt="The UE monogram">

A heavy **U** holds two bars that complete an **E** against its left arm:
*Universal Entropics*. The U's base and the two bars make three
horizontal strokes, one for each value of a trit. The middle stroke,
zero, is the short one and carries the accent. Read that way, the mark is
the balanced ternary digits standing on one another.

Construction, on a grid of 200 units: the arms are 38 units wide; the bowl
is a half-circle of outer radius 76; the bars are 30 units deep, the top
one 58 units long and the middle one 40. Keep clear space of half the
mark's width on every side.

## Colour

| name | use | value |
|---|---|---|
| Process black | the mark, the lettering | `#1b1b1b` |
| Entropic amber | the zero stroke; one accent word | `#e8962e`, the colour of a lit +1 lamp on the console |
| Card stock | backgrounds | `#f4efe4` |

The mark may be printed in black alone. In that case the zero stroke is
black too, and the mark still reads, because the zero stroke is the short
one.

## Lettering

A bold Swiss grotesque (Akzidenz-Grotesk in the original sheets; Helvetica
is acceptable), set in capitals and letterspaced. The house name sits
above a rule the full width of the lettering, and the section's name sits
below it. SEATTLE, in amber, is the only word in colour.
